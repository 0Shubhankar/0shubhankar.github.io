This interactive 3D visualization helps you explore how different parts of a city are connected based on their similarities. Imagine that each point on the globe represents a different part of the city—such as neighborhoods, landmarks, or important facilities.

What you're seeing includes labeled points where each point on the globe corresponds to a city element. These could be different locations or key parts of the city. The labels help identify them. The connecting lines between points represent relationships or similarities between these city elements. The closer or more connected two points are, the stronger the relationship, which is shown through thicker or brighter lines.

Exploring connections is simple: Move your mouse over any point on the globe, and you'll see the lines connecting it to other points get highlighted. This shows which elements are more closely related to each other. You can use your mouse to zoom in and out or rotate the globe to explore different areas. This makes it easy to focus on specific regions or connections within the city.

Customization options are available through the control panel on the side. You can adjust the size of the globe, the font size of the labels, and even change the colors of the lines and text to match your preferences. The visualization is drawing relationships between city elements based on similarity data. The stronger the similarity between two points, the more prominent the line between them. By interacting with the globe, you can see how different areas of the city are connected, revealing clusters or patterns you might not expect.
