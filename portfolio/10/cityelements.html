Welcome to the City Relationship Explorer

This interactive 3D visualization helps you explore how different parts of a city are connected based on their similarities. Each point on the globe represents a different part of the city, such as neighborhoods, landmarks, or important facilities.

What You’re Seeing:

- Labeled Points: Each point on the globe corresponds to a city element. These could be different locations or key parts of the city. The labels help identify them.
- Connecting Lines: The lines between points represent relationships or similarities between these city elements. The closer or more connected two points are, the stronger the relationship, which is shown through thicker or brighter lines.

How It Works:

1. Exploring Connections: Move your mouse over any point on the globe, and you'll see the lines connecting it to other points get highlighted. This shows which elements are more closely related to each other.
2. Zoom and Rotate: Use your mouse to zoom in and out or rotate the globe to explore different areas. This makes it easy to focus on specific regions or connections within the city.
3. Customization: Using the control panel on the side, you can adjust the size of the globe, font size, and even the colors of the lines and text.
