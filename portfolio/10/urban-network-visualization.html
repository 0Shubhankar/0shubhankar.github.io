Urban Networks: Understanding the Connections That Shape Cities

Cities are more than collections of buildings and places. They are systems of relationships. This interactive 3D network explores similarities between urban elements, revealing how individual components can form interconnected groups and patterns.

Nodes represent urban elements Lines show recorded similarities Hover to reveal connections

The visualization represents urban elements as labeled points distributed across a three-dimensional sphere. Connecting lines illustrate relationships derived from the underlying similarity dataset. Hovering over an element highlights its recorded connections, making it easier to explore how individual parts relate to the wider system.

This network-based perspective offers a different way to investigate the structure of cities. Rather than examining elements independently, it encourages exploration of relationships, connected groups and potential patterns. The sphere is an abstract arrangement, not a geographical representation of actual locations or distances.
