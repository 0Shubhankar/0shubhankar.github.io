Gotham is facing a growing homelessness crisis as rising rents and a lack of affordable housing push more residents onto the streets. The city needs immediate and long-term solutions to address this urgent issue.

One approach is to Expand Emergency Shelter and Transitional Housing, offering quick relief by providing temporary housing to those in need. This ensures that the homeless population has a safe place to stay while they work toward a more permanent solution.

The second option is to Develop Permanent Supportive Housing (PSH), which combines long-term housing with supportive services like mental health care, job training, and substance abuse programs.
