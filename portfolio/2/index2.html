Traffic in Key Event Spaces in Manhattan

Explore the impact of major event spaces on Manhattan traffic. Each location is known for hosting significant events, which affect traffic patterns in the area. Learn about these locations, the types of events they host, and the typical traffic levels during and outside these events. Visualizations and data insights provide a comprehensive overview of how these spaces influence urban mobility.

Use the sliders to explore traffic patterns based on different days of the week and times of the day.

Times Square

Times Square is iconic for its bright lights, Broadway theaters, and heavy pedestrian traffic. This bustling commercial and tourist hub significantly impacts traffic patterns.

  • Events: New Year's Eve, street performances, Broadway shows.
  • Traffic Levels: Very High (3000-4000 vehicles/hour) during events; High (1000-1500 vehicles/hour) otherwise.

Madison Square Garden

Madison Square Garden is a multipurpose indoor arena in Midtown Manhattan, home to sports games, concerts, and large events. It draws substantial crowds, affecting traffic flow in the surrounding area.

  • Events: NBA games, concerts, conventions.
  • Traffic Levels: High (2000-3000 vehicles/hour) during events; Moderate (500-1000 vehicles/hour) otherwise.

Lincoln Center

Lincoln Center is renowned for its cultural performances, including opera, ballet, symphony, and theater. It attracts art enthusiasts, influencing traffic patterns, especially during major performances.

  • Events: Opera, ballet, theater performances.
  • Traffic Levels: High (1500-2000 vehicles/hour) during events; Moderate (500-700 vehicles/hour) otherwise.

Central Park

Central Park is a vast urban park hosting outdoor concerts, festivals, and marathons. It serves as a green oasis in the city, but large events can cause significant traffic disruptions.

  • Events: Concerts, festivals, marathons.
  • Traffic Levels: High (1500-2000 vehicles/hour) during events; Low (200-500 vehicles/hour) otherwise.

Rockefeller Center

Rockefeller Center is famous for its seasonal attractions, including ice skating and the annual Christmas tree lighting. These events draw large crowds and can lead to increased traffic congestion.

  • Events: Ice skating, Christmas tree lighting.
  • Traffic Levels: High (1500-2500 vehicles/hour) during events; Moderate (500-800 vehicles/hour) otherwise.

Javits Center

The Javits Center is a major convention center in Manhattan, hosting conferences, trade shows, and exhibitions. Large events here can significantly impact traffic in the surrounding areas.

  • Events: Trade shows, conventions, exhibitions.
  • Traffic Levels: High (2000-2500 vehicles/hour) during events; Low to Moderate (300-600 vehicles/hour) otherwise.

Broadway Theaters

Broadway Theaters are the heart of American theater, hosting numerous performances. The concentration of theaters in this area leads to significant traffic, especially around showtimes.

  • Events: Broadway shows, theater performances.
  • Traffic Levels: Moderate to High (1000-1500 vehicles/hour) during events; Moderate (500-800 vehicles/hour) otherwise.
