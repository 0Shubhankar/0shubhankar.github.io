# Greenville GIE 0.1 — standalone prototype
Node 18+, no dependencies. `npm test`; `node demo.js`.
Original source: https://github.com/0shubhankar/0shubhankar.github.io/blob/main/portfolio/20/green-space-policy-game.html. story.js retains decision IDs and branches with paraphrased labels; forced restarts are intentionally converted to continuation. Three chapters, two illustrative annual periods per chapter; 96 routes. First choice sets branch only, with no direct physical effect.

Units: hectares, population, fictional dollar millions, annual periods. Initial state: three equal-area neighbourhoods, 100,000 people, 128 ha public parks, $24m dedicated policy reserve. Park creation transfers free land to park at $0.22m/ha; condition is area-weighted with new land at 0.95. Tree interventions are canopy percentage-point increments (illustrative, not actual tree counts). Each year population grows 0.8%, free land converts to development at 3 ha/zone times commercial restriction factor. Maintenance need $0.025m/ha/year; volunteer service is capped at 65% of need, stipend and maintenance payments capped by cash. Condition changes +0.035*funding ratio -0.09*(1-ratio), bounded 0.1–1. Annual receipts include $1.1m baseline, net tax and maintenance sponsorship. Capital grants, sponsorship, fundraising and redirected funds use explicit fictional amounts. Redirected funding is tracked as an opportunity cost, not a model of other infrastructure. Cash reconciles opening reserve + receipts - spending; land categories reconcile.

Access proxy = 100*(1-exp(-park sqm per resident/12))*park condition; gap = maximum minus minimum zone proxy. This is NOT measured walking access or percent of population within walking distance. Canopy is area-weighted percent. Governance proxy = 100*clamp(0.5+0.25*engagement-0.4*corporateControl,0,1), not survey evidence. No cooling, biodiversity, physical transport network, land market, jobs, or real fiscal incidence model yet. Partnerships and grants have fixed hypothetical receipts, not stochastic success. Maintenance shortage affects condition; no arrears accounting. New park construction is immediate, not a construction schedule.

Assessment compares matched six-year no-policy simulation, with 50 neutral. Component score = clamp(50+50*delta/scale,0,100). Scales: low-income access 25 points, gap reduction 25 points, condition 30 points, development 60 ha, fiscal 24m (cash minus redirected funds relative to baseline), governance 40 points. Weights 25/25/20/10/10/10 percent. These are gameplay assumptions, not validated welfare metrics. All coefficients are illustrative. No UUE integration or browser UI yet. Original public source is unchanged.

API: initial(), choose(state, sourceBranchId), indicators(state), assessment(state), baseline(year), advance(state,years), validate(state). choose returns an independent state; advance mutates its input. tree and paths() exported by story.js. History stores before/after indicators for every choice. Test output and route summary included.
