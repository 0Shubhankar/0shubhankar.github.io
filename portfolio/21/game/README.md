# Spudsville Under Siege — cinematic prototype 0.2
Local Vite entry at /p21-cinematic/. Uses the shared CityEngine directly and bundles the independent CPE 0.1 source without changing the shared source or other projects. Three original narrative challenges, 96 adapted routes, six fictional model years. Forced restart branches remain adapted to continue into review. Night default, high activity, no internal branding or numbered titles. Camera anchors use existing roads, buildings and parks; no actual police simulation or crime hotspots are rendered. Policy descriptions show nominal illustrative index increments, subject to bounds. Reports distinguish estimated and reported incidents and compare against the matched no-policy scenario. No empirical calibration, spatial crime predictions, surveillance placement or actual stakeholder survey data. Browser rendering and local UUE compatibility require verification. Tests: cd p21-cinematic/cpe && npm test. Launch from UUE root: npm run dev -- --port 5180 --strictPort.

## 0.2.1 police visual cues
Stationary representative officers and cars, steady emissive roof bars. Policy dependent, capped, reset on replay. No crime or patrol routing model. Safe-site search may omit props when no suitable site is found.

## 0.3 consolidated update
Police car geometry is scaled 1.6x (7.04 model units long), with red/blue emissive bars, translucent halos and local point lights. Actual UUE vehicle match and lighting are not verified in a browser; stationary models are not moving traffic. Larger clearance and water checks reduce placement conflicts. Synthetic equal-population districts allocate citywide incidents using modest capacity-related weights; no real geographic predictions or demographic risk classifications. Additional capacity is allocated equally except for a modest community-engagement emphasis. Annual incremental operating costs (millions): .012 per police capacity point above baseline, .006 per monitoring point above baseline, .004 per prevention commitment point, .003 per oversight point above baseline. Charges are limited to cash available; unpaid obligations are tracked and reduce the fiscal score. Police capacity effect is reduced when operating costs are unpaid; other policy effects are simplified. Stakeholder scores are benchmark-relative proxies, not survey results. No empirical calibration. All original decision routes preserved. Shared UUE unchanged.
