X:   Issue: Stakeholder Conflict in Downtown Revitalization

Mayor  is facing a critical juncture in the revitalization of downtown . Multiple stakeholder groups—including property owners, local businesses, residents, and political factions—are clashing over the future direction of the downtown area. Each group has its own priorities and vision, leading to conflicts that stall progress.



Option X1: Establish a Downtown Revitalization Advisory Board

Approach: Form a diverse board comprising representatives from each stakeholder group to provide input on revitalization projects and policies. This board would meet regularly to discuss plans, address concerns, and find common ground.

Pros:

Creates a formal platform for dialogue, potentially easing tensions by giving all parties a voice.
Helps ensure that the revitalization efforts consider a wide range of perspectives and needs.
Cons:

Could lead to prolonged discussions and slow decision-making if consensus is hard to achieve.
Risk of certain groups dominating the conversation, potentially sidelining smaller stakeholders.

Option X2: Conduct Comprehensive Stakeholder Impact Assessments

Approach: Before implementing any major changes, conduct thorough impact assessments to understand how different revitalization strategies affect various groups. This data-driven approach would inform policy decisions to maximize benefits and minimize adverse effects.

Pros:

Provides a clear, objective basis for decisions, which can be defended to stakeholders with concrete data.
Helps identify and mitigate potential negative impacts on specific groups before they become issues.
Cons:

Time-consuming and potentially costly to carry out detailed studies.
May delay urgent revitalization efforts while assessments are being completed.
Mayor Ledger needs to choose an approach that not only resolves the current conflicts but also paves the way for a more collaborative and efficient revitalization process. Each option offers a method to manage the diverse interests and lead downtown Metroville toward a prosperous future.
