Understanding the model

Who can still afford to live here?

The six neighbourhood types retain the original project's illustrative base rents, property values, and three macroeconomic scenarios. The map uses its existing building footprints, but neighbourhood classifications are assigned using six reference locations—not observed household data. Hover or click to inspect a building.

Scenario-adjusted rent and prices

Rg,s = Rg,0(1 + δRg,s),   Pg,s = Pg,0(1 + δPg,s)

R and P denote monthly rent and property value. Subscript g identifies a neighbourhood type, s a scenario, and δ the original project's assumed percentage change. In the intervention scenario, the policy cap limits positive rent growth.

Housing cost burden

βg,s = max(0, Rg,s − a) / [Yg,0(1 + γ)]

β is monthly rent as a share of income, a is optional rental assistance, and γ is the income adjustment. Baseline incomes are transparent illustrative assumptions added for affordability analysis, not observations from the mapped area. A 30% threshold is used as a simple screening indicator rather than a precise measure of hardship.

Reading the experiment

The preview opens in the economic-boom scenario so differences are immediately visible. A baseline scenario correctly produces zero rent and price changes, while other scenarios apply different assumed changes to each neighbourhood category. Blue represents a decline, grey no change, and orange-red an increase. Switch between affordability, rent change, property-price change, and neighbourhood classification. The same buildings stay fixed while scenario outcomes change. Use the comparison bars to see which neighbourhood types carry the greatest burden, or test whether rental assistance offsets a rise in housing costs.

This is a deterministic scenario illustration, not a calibrated forecast. Economic growth, interest rates, unemployment, and inflation are scenario descriptions rather than independently estimated causal coefficients. Building footprints do not establish actual neighbourhood income or rent.
