Scenario 1: Economic Boom with Rising Interest Rates

Assumptions: Interest rates increase from 3% to 6% to control inflation, coupled with strong GDP growth of 4% annually, a drop in unemployment to 3%, and an inflation rise to 5% per year.

Expected Outcomes: In luxury neighborhoods, property prices soar by 12% annually due to heightened demand, with rents climbing 8%. This leads to an uptick in high-end real estate investments. Upper-middle-class areas see property prices grow by 10% annually, with rent increases of 7%, slightly reducing affordability. Middle-class neighborhoods experience a 6% rise in property prices and a 5% increase in rent, pushing some residents towards more affordable areas. Lower-middle-class areas face a 4% property price increase and a similar rent hike, heightening gentrification risks. Working-class neighborhoods see a modest 3% growth in both property prices and rent, increasing the demand for rental assistance. Low-income areas witness a 2% annual increase in both property prices and rent, exacerbating financial strain.

Scenario 2: Economic Recession with Falling Interest Rates

Assumptions: Interest rates fall from 4% to 1% to stimulate the economy, which contracts by -1% annually, with unemployment rising to 8% and inflation dropping to 1%.

Expected Outcomes: Luxury neighborhoods experience a 5% annual decline in property prices and a slight decrease in rent, stalling new luxury developments. Upper-middle-class areas see a 4% drop in property values and a 2% rent reduction, increasing landlord competition. Middle-class property prices decrease by 3%, but rent remains stable as families postpone buying homes. Lower-middle-class areas also see property prices decrease by 3%, with a 1% rent reduction, causing some out-migration. Working-class neighborhoods have minimal changes in property values and stable rent, necessitating increased governmental support. Low-income areas see a 2% decrease in property prices and a slight rent reduction, with heightened demand for social services.

Scenario 3: Targeted Rent Control and Subsidized Housing Development During Stagnant Economy

Assumptions: The economy shows zero growth, with stable interest rates at 3%, unemployment at 6%, and inflation at 2%. Rent control caps rent increases at 2% annually, with significant investment in subsidized housing for low-income and working-class neighborhoods.

Expected Outcomes: In luxury neighborhoods, property prices remain stagnant but rent increases are capped, maintaining current living standards. Upper-middle-class areas also see no growth in property prices, with stable rent due to rent control. Middle-class neighborhoods benefit from stable property prices and capped rent increases, maintaining affordability. Lower-middle-class areas see no property price growth but enjoy stable rent, reducing gentrification risks. Working-class neighborhoods experience a slight increase in property values and stable rent due to government subsidies, improving housing availability. Low-income areas see a small rise in property prices and stable rent, enhancing housing access and reducing financial stress.

Limitations of the Study: This study, while comprehensive, presents a limited number of predefined economic scenarios and their impacts on various neighborhood classes. The scenarios provided are simplified models that do not account for all possible variables and their interdependencies.
