Comprehensive Overview of the Leaflet-Based Mapping Application:

This web-based mapping application leverages the Leaflet JavaScript library to enable users to visually explore and understand the impact of various urban factors on housing prices across different neighborhoods. Users can interactively select any building on the map to set it as the primary workplace location. This action recalibrates the map to reflect updated socio-economic data, showing how housing prices adjust based on the proximity to the workplace, local traffic congestion levels, and the quality of public transportation.

User Interface and Interaction Design:

The application features a full-screen map interface against a stark black background, emphasizing the colorful data points and interactive elements. A control panel positioned on the top right of the screen includes sliders for adjusting elasticity, congestion, and public transport quality, each equipped with tooltips for guidance.

Dynamic Data Visualization:

When a user selects a new workplace location, the app calculates the distance from this new center to various points of interest on the map, incorporating a randomized factor to simulate real-world irregularities in distance impact. The elasticity slider adjusts how responsive the housing prices are to changes in distance from the workplace. The congestion and public transport sliders influence housing prices by simulating the effects of local traffic conditions and the availability and quality of public transit.

Mathematical Formulations for Pricing:

The application calculates updated housing prices and rents using a multi-factor approach. The proximity price is calculated as Base Price × (1 - (Distance/Max Distance)) × Elasticity. The transportation factor is calculated as (Public Transport Quality/Distance) × (1 - Congestion Factor). The final adjusted price combines these factors, calculated as Proximity Price × (1 + (Transportation Factor/10)). Similarly, the updated rent is calculated using the base rent adjusted by the same transportation factor.

Conclusion:

This Leaflet-based application serves as an interactive tool for real estate professionals, urban planners, and the general public, providing insights into how urban dynamics like workplace location, congestion, and public transport quality can influence residential housing prices. The interactive adjustments and instant visualizations help users understand the complex interplay between urban living conditions and real estate economics.
