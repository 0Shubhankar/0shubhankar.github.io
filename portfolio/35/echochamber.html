This simulation shows how people form connections and the emergence of echo chambers and polarization based on factors like income, profession, political alignment, and other personal traits.

This visualization illustrates how social dynamics like echo chambers and polarization arise in a simulated population. By adjusting various parameters, users can explore how different social and economic factors influence the formation of these phenomena. Here’s how to interpret and understand the visualization:

Understanding Echo Chambers

What are Echo Chambers?
Echo chambers are social environments where people primarily connect with others who share similar beliefs, traits, or opinions. These connections reinforce their perspectives, limiting exposure to differing viewpoints.

Understanding Polarization

What is Polarization?
Polarization occurs when groups or individuals hold opposing views or characteristics, creating division. Politically, polarization means individuals with different political views rarely connect or interact.
