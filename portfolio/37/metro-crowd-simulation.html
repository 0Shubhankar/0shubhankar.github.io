This simulation models a metro train station environment where various elements such as trains, tracks, and colorful moving dots (representing people) come together to depict the flow and behavior of passengers. Each component plays a specific role:

Trains and Tracks: These elements outline the station's layout and simulate the basic mechanics of train movements, which helps contextualize the passenger flow within the station.

Moving Dots (Passengers): The different colored dots represent passengers, with colors indicating their actions: red for exiting and cyan for boarding. This animation illustrates the routes passengers take, visualizing the flow and density of the crowd.

Stairs and Gaps: These features represent the structural aspects of the station that influence passenger movement. They help segment the station, demonstrating how passengers navigate physical barriers and transitions between tracks.

Metrics Tracking: The simulation quantifies elements such as crowd density, waiting times, and platform congestion in real-time based on the movement of the dots. This functionality provides immediate insights into how changes in train schedules or passenger behavior might impact these metrics.

Train Arrival Timer: A countdown timer updates every second to indicate when the next train will arrive or depart. This element is essential for illustrating how the timing of train arrivals affects passenger flow and station congestion.

In summary, this simulation provides a dynamic representation of train station operations, allowing users to visualize passenger flow and the effects of various factors on transit efficiency.
