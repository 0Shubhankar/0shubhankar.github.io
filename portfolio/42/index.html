05 / THE MATH UNDER THE HOOD

No magic multipliers.

Project cash flow

Revenue − operating costs − maintenance capital expenditure − cash taxes = unlevered cash flow.

Debt schedule

Annual debt service = interest on beginning loan balance + scheduled straight-line principal; remaining debt declines annually.

DCF value

Expected future project cash flows discounted at a risk-adjusted rate, plus discounted terminal value; deduct outstanding debt for equity value.

Municipal bond value

Present value of remaining fixed coupons and principal at the current required yield; duration is computed by repricing around that yield.

Debt service coverage

Cash available for debt service divided by annual scheduled interest and principal, for cash-generating infrastructure holdings.

Return & risk

Portfolio NAV, annualized NAV return, concentration, sensitivity to interest rates and a distribution of scenario shocks.

This is a deliberately simplified financial simulation, not an investment recommendation, tax model, credit rating or accurate forecast. Forecasts and terminal multiples are assumptions. Asset earnings, bonds and debt accounting are kept separate.
