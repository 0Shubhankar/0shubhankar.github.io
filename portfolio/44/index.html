✳IT'S A GAME. THE MATH ISN'T PRETEND.

Fictional parcels, fictional markets, and simplified but consistent cash-flow and debt logic. Estimated property values are not cash. Construction loans must be repaid. A positive NPV does not prevent a liquidity crisis.

HOW TO PLAY

Buy a parcel → choose a building → choose leverage → fund construction → collect rent, sell, or refinance. Advance the year to see the consequences. The game ends after year 10, or earlier if you decide to start over.

THE HOUSE RULES

All amounts are in fictional US$ millions. Loans are simplified, fixed-rate structures; there are no taxes, legal fees, transaction-level waterfall, or real-world lending offers. This is an educational simulation, not financial advice.
