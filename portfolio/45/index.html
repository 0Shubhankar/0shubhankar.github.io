TREASURY NOTEBOOK

A playable, educational simulation with fictional forecasts, credit spreads and disaster impacts. Borrowing, insurance and capital spending have modeled cash consequences; no real securities, insurance, weather forecasts or public credit ratings are represented.

OPERATIONS MANUAL

FOUR SEASONS.
ONE CITY.

Each season follows seven short steps. You can inspect the map, treasury and accounting notebook at any time.

  1. Forecast — read the storm warning and the city’s condition.
  2. Budget — set taxes and everyday public service spending.
  3. Finance — decide whether to borrow to protect your city.
  4. Protect — fund drainage, flood walls or emergency shelters.
  5. Storm — weather strikes (or misses); uncertainty is real.
  6. Recover — rebuild quickly, slowly, or prioritize essential services.
  7. Close the books — reconcile cash, debt and resident outcomes.

Credit grades and storm probabilities are fictional game mechanics. There is no single perfect ending: your goal is to protect both people and the public purse.
