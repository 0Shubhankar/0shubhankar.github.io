THE RULES OF THE ROOM
01 / BUY

Click a property on the city board. Compare the asking price with income-based value. Choose an offer, lender structure, and buyer.

02 / OPERATE

Retain tenants, improve properties, sell buildings or refinance. Every action changes company cash and risk.

03 / TURN THE YEAR

Collect rents, pay expenses and debt service, experience a market event, and inspect the reconciled cash ledger.

04 / UNLOCK

Later chapters add an investor fund, interest-rate protection and securities linked to the mortgage market.

All figures, properties, events and securities are fictional. Values are estimates, not appraisals. Funds are legally and financially separate from your company in the simulation.
