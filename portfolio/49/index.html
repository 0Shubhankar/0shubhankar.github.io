YOUR FIELD GUIDE

HOW TO RIDE
THE MARKET

There is no hidden trick. This is a sequence of five connected experiments using a fictional city and explicitly defined financial mathematics.

  1. Move the main rate dial. The whole yield curve responds, with separate choices for its shape and credit spreads.
  2. Inspect bonds. Compare different payment structures, duration and convexity.
  3. Finance the city. Existing fixed debt stays fixed; new borrowing reprices and must fit the city budget.
  4. Choose a hedge. See the cash-flow trade-offs of a swap or interest-rate cap.
  5. Launch a crisis. Run a three-year scenario and examine a linked liquidity and valuation outcome.

All monetary amounts are simulated. A real city or bank would need a much deeper model and independently validated data.

THE MATH LAB

These are the exact principles behind the interactive model. Model assumptions are deliberately separated from financial identities.

01 / SPOT DISCOUNT FACTORDF(t) = 1 / (1 + zₜ)ᵗ

zₜ is the modeled annual spot rate for maturity t, with annual compounding.

02 / BOND VALUEP = Σ CFₜ × DF(t)

Coupon and principal payments are discounted using their relevant modeled spot rates, plus a credit spread for non-government issuers.

03 / DURATION & CONVEXITYDuration ≈ (P₋ − P₊) / (2 × P × Δy)Convexity ≈ (P₋ + P₊ − 2P) / (P × Δy²)

Calculated with full revaluation after ±10 basis-point parallel rate shifts; not used for a floating-rate note's reset dynamics alone.

04 / PROPERTY VALUATIONAsset value = sustainable NOI / cap rate

NOI is net operating income before financing; cap rate responds to the 10-year benchmark and credit conditions based on a stated scenario rule.

05 / MUNICIPAL DEBT COVERAGEDSCR = (revenue − operating cost) / scheduled debt service

Debt service includes coupons and scheduled principal. An outstanding principal amount that matures must be refinanced or paid with cash.

06 / SWAP & CAPSwap net coupon cost = notional × (fixed swap rate + loan spread)Cap payoffₜ = notional × max(referenceₜ − strike, 0)

A pay-fixed swap receives the modeled reference rate and pays a locked fixed rate. A cap requires an upfront premium; both are simplified five-year illustrations.

07 / THREE-YEAR CITY TREASURYClosing cash = opening cash + revenue − operating cost − debt service − capital expenditure + new borrowing

Financial results separate operating cash, debt proceeds, interest expense, and principal flows. A credit shock increases refinancing costs, not the coupon on legacy fixed-rate bonds.
