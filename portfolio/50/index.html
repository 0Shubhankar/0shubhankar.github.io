THE MATHEMATICS BEHIND THE MAGIC

THE RULES OF TWO FUTURES

All figures are fictional. Both cities begin with identical populations, revenues, reserves, housing conditions, infrastructure assets, and outstanding bond tranches. The same deterministic event sequence affects both, allowing comparisons of different policies under a controlled scenario.

01 · Treasury cash

Closing cash = opening cash + operating receipts − service spending − existing debt principal and interest − capital projects + gross new borrowing − issuance fees + value-capture receipts − shock repair spending + emergency borrowing. When the treasury would fall below zero, the model draws an explicitly recorded high-cost emergency note. This is a simplified credit backstop, not a prediction of market access.

02 · Bond pricing and issuance

New issues have fixed coupons, 12-year straight-line principal repayment, and no first-year coupon because proceeds are assumed issued at year-end. Legacy bonds amortize over twenty years. A city's coupon equals a scenario base rate plus a simplified fiscal spread and any instrument-specific premium. Digital issuance is a hypothetical permissioned ledger, not a live blockchain. Its administrative costs may be higher or lower depending on issuance volume and onboarding fees.

03 · The city changes

Transit raises access and may increase nearby rents; housing adds units and moderates rent growth; water investment improves services; resilience reduces modeled storm losses. Taxes influence revenues and the job-growth assumption. Service spending influences service quality and economic activity. The demand, supply and behavior relationships are deliberately stylized.

04 · Housing burden

Housing burden = annualized median asking rent ÷ modeled median household income. It is a simplified stress indicator, not the percentage of actual households paying more than 30% of income toward housing. Land-value capture is modeled as a one-time development levy rather than automatically turning all property appreciation into government cash.

05 · What the model does not claim

The city accounts are a cash-flow simulation, not audited governmental fund statements or a complete accrual three-statement model. Debt spreads are illustrative rather than observed bond yields. Infrastructure capital stock is a simplified physical proxy. No probability distribution or causal claim is estimated from real data. Comparing outcomes demonstrates consequences of the game's assumptions, not what must occur in the real world.
