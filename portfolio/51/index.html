FIELD NOTES

Listen a little closer.

001

Why sound?

Cities are heard as well as seen. Street design, mobility and nature contribute to our sense of place. This installation invites you to play with those ingredients rather than rank neighborhoods by a single score.

002

What am I hearing?

Original procedural audio made with the browser's Web Audio API: filtered noise, synthesized bells, footsteps and bird-like tones. These are expressive abstractions, not authentic location recordings or acoustic simulations.

003

What does it measure?

The frequency bars and waveform respond to the generated output. The interactive faders are relative mix controls, not decibel measurements. No microphone, sensor, recording of the surrounding world, or external data service is used.
