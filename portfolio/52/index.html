Notes from the material archive

This is an artistic interactive demonstration, not an environmental product declaration or engineering specification.

All comparative figures in data/materials.json are fictional example inputs per square metre of a notional finished wall. Service life is an assumed scenario, not a guarantee; transport distance is contextual, not an intrinsic material property. Carbon shares are illustrative lifecycle-stage allocations, not measured results.

A rigorous edition should fix wall thickness, structural and thermal performance, locality, time horizon and system boundaries; use EPDs or peer-reviewed LCAs; incorporate binders, mortars and construction waste; and report uncertainty.
